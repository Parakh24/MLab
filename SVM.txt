Linear Kernel = Simple , Linearly separable Data 


Polynomial Kernel = Expands the feature space by adding polynomial combinations of features
  When to use:
     If the data in 2D is circular or follows a quadratic curve, a polynomial kernel can separate it. 
     Works well for problems where the boundary is polynomial-shaped.

RBF(Gaussian Kernel) = can create very complex, non-linear decision boundaries.
  When to use:
      Default choice when you don't know the data pattern
      Handles highly non-linear problem.
