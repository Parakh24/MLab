Pros: 

Easy to interpret and visualize. 
Handles numerical + categorical data. 
No scaling/normalization required 

Cons: 

Prone to overfitting (trees can grow very deep) 

Small changes in data can change the tree drastically because it greedily picks the best feature & 
threshold at each node based only on the current subset of data.

Changing one value can change which split is "best" at the root.

Since all subsequent splits depend on the previous splits, the entire tree structure may change.

Greedy splitting may not always find the optimal tree.



Decision trees are used for both classification and regression: 

  1. Classification(discrete value): 
          metrics used like: 
              Entropy + Information Gain
              Gini Impurity 

  2. Regression(continuous values): 
          metrics used like: 
              Mean Squared Error(MSE)
              Mean Absolute Error(MAE) 
              Variance Reduction
